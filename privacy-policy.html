2. Information We May Collect

When our services become operational, we may collect:

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address, company details, and phone number when users register or interact with our tools.

3. How We May Use Your Data

When our services become operational, we may process your information for:

  • Providing and maintaining our services

4. Data Security

We will implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, including encryption for data in transit and at rest. While we will make reasonable efforts to maintain high cybersecurity standards, no method of transmission over the internet can be 100% secure.

5. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected individuals without undue delay and report the breach to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

6. Data Sharing

  • Intra-Entity Data Transfer: Any data transfer between Keystone Digital Sdn. Bhd. and Keystone Intelligence Global will be limited to anonymised insights and aggregated statistical trends. We will not transfer, share, or expose raw personal details across entities.
  • Disclosure to Authorities: We may disclose data to relevant government authorities when legally necessary to comply with Malaysian laws.
  • No Sale of Data: We will not sell, trade, or rent your personally identifiable information to third-party marketers.

7. Your Rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (and its 2024 amendments), you retain the right to access, correct, and withdraw consent for processing of your personal data.
